# Maine construction and trade credential audit

Audit baseline: `origin/main` at `a80c60b28bd36b0f0f0bbcb8c2ef2433061fc048`. This is source research, not a license census.

## Existing Maine coverage

No Maine state page or Maine-specific credential dataset was found in the baseline. Existing general contractor and trades search functions do not establish statewide Maine licensure.

## Statewide credential classes

- Electricians' Examining Board licenses master, journeyman, limited, apprentice, journeyman-in-training, and helper electricians. These are person credential classes, not a business contractor census.
- Plumbers' Examining Board licenses master, journeyman, trainee, and journeyman-in-training plumbers. These are separate person credential classes.
- Maine PFR's professions directory also identifies potentially construction-related professions including architects, professional engineers, elevator mechanics, boiler operators, and fuel technicians. Legal scope and class-level roster acquisition remain to be established before publication.
- Reviewed sources do not establish a universal statewide general-contractor license. Do not create a fictitious universal census. Exclude Portland/local licensing.

## Source acquisition status

Both electrician and plumber official services advertise downloadable licensee lists. The official PFR portal currently loops between the license-search endpoint and its cookie-detection redirect when requested in this acquisition environment; no bulk rows have been acquired. Person rows, identifiers, license classes, statuses, source clocks, and distinct IDs remain UNKNOWN, not zero.
