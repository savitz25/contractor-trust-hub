# Colorado contractor sources (CO-CON-001)

Official primary source: Colorado Information Marketplace / DORA Professional and Occupational Licenses (`7s5z-vewr`) and license-type dictionary (`349y-twqi`).

Colorado does **not** issue a statewide general-contractor license. Statewide contractor-business evidence is:

- EC Electrical Contractor (business)
- PC Plumbing Contractor (business)

Individual trades (ME/JW/RW/MP/JP/RP) are person-grain. Apprentices (APE/AP) are internal. AELS (PE/ARC/PLS/LA) are professional credentials, not GC businesses.

Canonical identity: `CO-DORA:{licensePrefix}:{licenseNumber}`.

Discipline attaches only by that exact ID. Name-only is unsafe.

Denver local licenses/permits are parked. Bond/insurance bulk is not acquired (unknown, not zero).
