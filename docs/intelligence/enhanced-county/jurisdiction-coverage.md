# Jurisdiction coverage matrix — Broward & Palm Beach

Rule: never say “Broward County permits” or “Palm Beach County permits” until the source’s AHJ is proven.

ePermits OneStop is a **submittal hub** for municipal applications plus **associated county** approvals. It is **not** complete countywide historical permit coverage.

## Palm Beach County

PZB Building Open Permit Search: **HARD unincorporated** (PCN prefix `00`). Municipalities are separate AHJs. Census BPS footnote: unincorporated system **also covers Loxahatchee Groves**; **Westlake** has its own system since 2017.
